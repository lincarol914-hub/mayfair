About Ming Xuan

Fifteen Years of Craft

Ming Xuan has called Mayfair home since 2009, founded by Head Chef Chen and his team. We believe true Chinese cooking is an art of heat, season and fellowship.

From the morning's market picks to duck slow-roasted over charcoal, we blend traditional technique with a modern sensibility — so every guest tastes a little of home.
